# Conjectures and falsification targets

Every conjecture in this repository should have a corresponding falsification strategy.

## C1 — Bilateral collapse separation

**Conjecture.** There is a natural four-valued lift of the Scott system in which `MC+` and `MC-` are independent.

**Falsification target.** Show that the remaining axioms force `MC+ ↔ MC-` under every acceptable lift.

## C2 — Asymmetric rigidity dependence

**Conjecture.** `R+` and `R-` have different consequences for modal collapse.

**Falsification target.** Prove that they are interderivable given the other accepted axioms.

## C3 — Glut/gap asymmetry for positivity

**Conjecture.** `P(φ)=B` and `P(φ)=N` propagate differently through Godlikeness and possible exemplification.

**Falsification target.** Show that the chosen semantics collapses the two statuses for all Gödel-relevant formulas.

## C4 — Non-trivial inconsistent models

**Conjecture.** There are models with some inconsistent positivity assignments in which the theory remains non-trivial and at least part of the Gödel theorem chain survives.

**Falsification target.** Show that any relevant positivity glut forces triviality or destroys every existence result.

## C5 — Classical recovery

**Conjecture.** Restricting all relevant formulas to classical values and imposing the appropriate modal constraints recovers the selected Scott baseline.

**Falsification target.** Exhibit a classical-valued model in which the lifted axioms diverge from the baseline.
