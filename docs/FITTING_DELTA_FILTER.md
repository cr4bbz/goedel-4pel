# Gate 8 — Four-valued Fitting delta-filter on profile fixed points

**Version:** `fitting-delta-filter-v0.1`  
**Status:** Lean 4.30.0 build green; finite separation regression green  
**Scope:** a conservative FDE generalization of the classical extensional `delta`-filter, located on the admissible fixed-point algebra of profile saturation  
**Literature anchor:** Benzmüller–Fuenmayor 2020, Section 3.2 and the Fitting ultrafilter analysis

This milestone follows `fitting-entailment-v0.1`. The source definition treats a classical `delta`-filter as a family of positive rigid extensions containing the universal extension, excluding the empty extension, upward closed under subset, and closed under intersection. A `delta`-ultrafilter adds maximality by deciding each extension or its complement.

The bilateral lift must first answer which of the two FDE orders replaces ordinary subset inclusion.

## 1. The two orders must not be conflated

The earlier profile closure uses bilateral support inclusion:

\[
X\preceq_kY
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
X^+\subseteq Y^+
\land
X^-\subseteq Y^-.
\]

This is the FDE information order: both information channels grow upwards.

A lattice filter belongs instead to the truth order:

\[
X\preceq_tY
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
X^+\subseteq Y^+
\land
Y^-\subseteq X^-.
\]

Positive support grows upwards while negative support shrinks. Lean now proves that pointwise FDE conjunction and disjunction are respectively meet and join for this order, and that FDE negation reverses it.

Thus the profile closure and the filter use different orders for principled reasons:

```text
profile hull Sat_w:  information order
FDE delta-filter:    truth order
```

## 2. Admissible fixed-point algebra

`AdmissibleFDEAlgebra` requires the selected domain to contain FDE top and bottom and to be closed under FDE conjunction and disjunction. Negation closure is already part of `AdmissibleSemantics`.

`ProfileDeltaFilterAdm` combines:

1. positive-profile saturation;
2. the admissible FDE subalgebra condition;
3. at every world, a proper positive-support filter in the truth order.

The filter laws are:

\[
+P(\top_F),
\qquad
\neg +P(\bot_F),
\]

\[
+P(X)\land X\preceq_tY
\Longrightarrow +P(Y),
\]

and

\[
+P(X)\land +P(Y)
\Longrightarrow +P(X\wedge_FY),
\]

with all carrier extensions required to be admissible.

By the previous fixed-point theorem, every admissible carrier element of a `ProfileDeltaFilterAdm` is fixed by `profileSaturateAt` up to bilateral extensional equivalence.

## 3. Classical recovery

Lean defines the direct classical order

\[
X\preceq_+Y
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
X^+\subseteq Y^+.
\]

Truth-order inclusion always implies positive inclusion. Under classical coherence of admissible extensions, the converse also holds because negative membership is the complement of positive membership. Consequently Lean proves:

\[
\boxed{
\text{FDE }\delta\text{-filter}
\iff
\text{classical positive-set }\delta\text{-filter}
}
\]

on the coherent fragment. The new definition is therefore a conservative extension of the source-level set filter rather than an unrelated bilattice construction.

## 4. Bilateral polarity and the dual ideal

`BilateralProfileDeltaFilterAdm` adds the exact admissible-domain A1 interface:

\[
-P(X)
\Longleftrightarrow
+P(\neg_FX).
\]

Lean derives both `A1LAdm` and `A1RAdm`. It also derives the dual ideal laws for negative support:

\[
-P(\bot_F),
\qquad
\neg -P(\top_F),
\]

downward closure in the truth order, and closure under FDE disjunction.

This polarity condition does not impose bivalence. Both

\[
+P(X)\land -P(X)
\]

and

\[
\neg +P(X)\land\neg -P(X)
\]

remain semantically possible.

## 5. Maximality is separate—and restores the old route

`ProfileDeltaUltrafilterAdm` adds:

\[
\forall X\in Adm,
\quad
+P(X)\lor +P(\neg_FX).
\]

This is exactly the complement-decision component isolated earlier. Lean therefore proves:

\[
\boxed{
\text{profile }\delta\text{-ultrafilter}
+CONS_G^{G,adm}
\Longrightarrow
COMP_P^{G,adm}
}
\]

and likewise with `A1-R` in place of relevant exemplification consistency.

The filter/ultrafilter distinction is thus dependency-theoretically real. The filter lives naturally on the quotient algebra; maximality returns to the classification route.

## 6. Finite strictness witness

`formal/finite/gate8_fitting_delta_filter.py` uses two entities and the 12-element pointwise subalgebra

\[
\{T,F,N\}\times\{T,F,N,B\}.
\]

It is closed under FDE negation, conjunction, and disjunction and contains genuine gaps and gluts. Positive support is the truth-order filter generated by

\[
(T,N).
\]

This filter contains only its generator and FDE top. The generator distinguishes the two entities, so positive-profile equivalence is identity and every admissible extension is profile-saturated.

The model satisfies:

- the admissible FDE-algebra conditions;
- profile saturation;
- the positive `delta`-filter laws;
- bilateral polarity;
- a positive Godlike witness `a`;
- relevant exemplification consistency.

But for the classical split extension

\[
A=(T,F)
\]

one has

\[
+A(a),
\qquad
\neg +P(A),
\qquad
\neg -P(A).
\]

Hence:

\[
\boxed{
\text{profile }\delta\text{-filter}
+CONS_G^{G,adm}
\not\Rightarrow
COMP_P^{G,adm}
}
\]

in this explicit finite model, while ultrafilter maximality plus consistency does imply `COMP` generally in Lean.

## 7. Current boundary

The four layers of the Fitting branch are now joined by a fifth, precisely typed interface:

```text
admissible profile fixed points
            +
FDE truth-order lattice structure
            +
proper positive delta-filter
            |
            | optional maximality
            v
delta-ultrafilter / complement decision / COMP route
```

The next questions are whether actualist entailment induces a more intrinsic preorder on this filter algebra, whether the filter can be transported to an explicit quotient type, and which completeness or principality assumptions force maximality.

No novelty claim is made before the Gate-9 prior-art audit.
