\section{Reconstructing the G\"odel--Scott Chain}
\label{sec:reconstruction}

\statusopen

With the propositional, modal, and initial positivity semantics now fixed, the next stage is to lift the remaining higher-order G\"odel--Scott machinery. The planned order remains deliberately incremental:
\[
  P
  \longrightarrow
  \text{A2 / possible exemplification}
  \longrightarrow
  G
  \longrightarrow
  \operatorname{Ess}
  \longrightarrow
  NE
  \longrightarrow
  \Box\exists^E x\,G(x).
\]

At each stage the aim is to classify not merely whether a theorem is derivable, but which information status its conclusion may receive and which assumptions control that status. In particular, gluts \(\Both\) and gaps \(\Neither\) in positivity must not be collapsed into a single notion of failure.

Gate 3 already fixes the four-valued positivity predicate together with the A1/A4 interfaces, but it deliberately stops short of a final object-language definition of Godlikeness. The temporary \(D1^+\) clause used in the local collapse analysis is only a support interface. Version 0.3 therefore leaves the final four-valued definitions of \(G\), essence, and necessary existence open, as well as the conditional policy required to lift A2 and the definitional occurrences of implication.
