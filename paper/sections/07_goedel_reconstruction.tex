\section{Reconstructing the G\"odel--Scott Chain}
\label{sec:reconstruction}

Gate 5 reconstructs the first higher-order branch of the Scott theory: A2, positive-property exemplification, A3, and Godlikeness. Essence and necessary existence remain open.

\subsection{Actualist quantification and necessary positive entailment}

I retain Scott's actualist quantification over individuals by using a classical metalanguage existence relation \(E(w,x)\). Positive and negative existential satisfaction are
\[
  w\models^+\exists^E x\,\varphi(x)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \exists x\,(E(w,x)\land w\models^+\varphi(x)),
\]
\[
  w\models^-\exists^E x\,\varphi(x)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \forall x\,(E(w,x)\Rightarrow w\models^-\varphi(x)),
\]
with the dual universal clauses. These reduce to ordinary actualist quantification on the classical fragment.

Rather than introduce an object-language four-valued conditional, define semantic necessary positive entailment by
\[
  \operatorname{NEnt}^{+}_{E}(\varphi,\psi,w)
\]
iff, at every world \(v\) accessible from \(w\), every actually existing positive instance of \(\varphi\) is a positive instance of \(\psi\). The Gate-5 lifting of A2 is
\[
  A2^+:\quad
  \pos{P(\varphi)}\land
  \operatorname{NEnt}^{+}_{E}(\varphi,\psi)
  \Rightarrow
  \pos{P(\psi)}.
\]
On the \(\{\True,\False\}\) fragment this recovers the truth-support content of Scott's classical A2.

\subsection{Truth-only positivity and possible exemplification}

Positive support and unopposed positivity must be distinguished. Define
\[
  \operatorname{Pos}_{T}(\varphi)
  \quad:\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \pos{P(\varphi)}
  \text{ and not }\neginfo{P(\varphi)},
\]
where ``not'' is metalanguage denial. Thus \(\operatorname{Pos}_{T}\) picks out value \(\True\) for the positivity statement rather than the designated pair \(\{\True,\Both\}\).

\begin{theorem}[Truth-only positive properties are possibly exemplified]
Assume globally \(A1_R\) and \(A2^+\). Then
\[
  \operatorname{Pos}_{T}(\varphi)
  \Rightarrow
  \pos{\Diamond\exists^E x\,\varphi(x)}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(w\) with \(\operatorname{Pos}_{T}(\varphi)\) and suppose, at the metalanguage level, that \(\pos{\Diamond\exists^E x\,\varphi(x)}\) fails. Then no accessible world contains an actually existing positive instance of \(\varphi\), so \(\operatorname{NEnt}^{+}_{E}(\varphi,\neg\varphi,w)\) holds vacuously. By \(A2^+\), \(\pos{P(\neg\varphi)}\). By \(A1_R\), \(\neginfo{P(\varphi)}\), contradicting the truth-only assumption. Hence positive possible exemplification follows.
\end{proof}

This result is denoted \(T1_T\). It reveals a directional division of labor inside Scott A1: \(A1_R\) drives the possible-exemplification branch, whereas \(A1_L\) drives the Gate-4 local reflection argument.

\paragraph{Glut countermodel.}
The stronger schema \(\pos{P(\varphi)}\Rightarrow\pos{\Diamond\exists^E x\,\varphi(x)}\) fails. Consider a one-world reflexive frame with an empty actual domain and assign \(\Both\) to every relevant positivity statement. Strong A1 and \(A2^+\) hold, while \(\pos{P(\varphi)}\) is true and the actualist existential has no positive witness. The classical reductio only generates the negative support already present in the positivity glut.

Thus the four-valued analogue of T1 naturally applies to unopposed, truth-only positivity rather than to arbitrary designated positivity.

\subsection{A3 and possible God-like existence}

Scott's A3 admits two conservative readings:
\[
  A3^+:\quad \pos{P(G)},
\]
which allows \(P(G)=\Both\), and the stronger Scott-control clause
\[
  A3_T:\quad \operatorname{Pos}_{T}(G).
\]
I retain \(A3^+\) as a robustness variant and adopt \(A3_T\) for the control reconstruction. From \(A3_T\) and \(T1_T\),
\[
  \boxed{
  \pos{\Diamond\exists^E x\,G(x)}
  }.
\]
This recovers the classical possibility branch without imposing global consistency on every positivity assignment.

\subsection{Support-based Godlikeness}

The Gate-5 control definition, \(G\text{-sup-v0.1}\), is bilateral:
\[
  w\models^+G(x)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \forall\varphi\,
  \bigl(w\models^+P(\varphi)\Rightarrow w\models^+\varphi(x)\bigr),
\]
\[
  w\models^-G(x)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \exists\varphi\,
  \bigl(w\models^+P(\varphi)\land w\models^-\varphi(x)\bigr).
\]
This immediately yields the previously abstract interface
\[
  D1^+:\quad
  \pos{G(x)}\land\pos{P(\varphi)}
  \Rightarrow
  \pos{\varphi(x)}.
\]
On the classical fragment the two clauses reduce to Scott's definition of Godlikeness.

The definition deliberately does not enforce consistency. All four information values remain possible for \(G(x)\): a positive property instantiated gluttily can make Godlikeness both positive and negative, while a positively supported positive property instantiated as \(\Neither\) can make Godlikeness neither positive nor negative.

\subsection{Audit of the Gate-4 collapse interfaces}

Gate 4 used \(D1^+\), \(GW\), and \(CONST\) as higher-order placeholders. Gate 5 resolves them asymmetrically.

First, \(D1^+\) follows directly from \(G\text{-sup-v0.1}\). Second, the full intensional property domain inherited from the HOL control setting supports lambda abstraction, so for every modal proposition \(\chi\) the constant property
\[
  Z_\chi:=\lambda x.\chi
\]
is available. Consequently the \(CONST\) interface is a higher-order comprehension assumption rather than an additional G\"odel axiom.

By contrast, \(GW\) is not yet available. A3 and T1 provide only possible positive God-like existence, not a positively supported God-like witness at every world. The required global witness supply belongs to the later T3 branch.

Nor do the remaining Gate-4 regularity assumptions follow from Godlikeness. Strong A1 still permits \(N/N\) gaps in positivity, so it does not yield \(COMP_P\). Likewise, if \(P(Z)=\Neither\) and \(Z(x)=\Both\), support-based Godlikeness places no constraint on \(Z\); therefore \(\pos{G(x)}\) and a glutty arbitrary exemplification can coexist, and \(CONS_G\) is not derived.

Thus Gate 5 reconstructs
\[
  A1_R+A2^++A3_T
  \Longrightarrow
  \pos{\Diamond\exists^E x\,G(x)},
\]
and discharges \(D1^+\) and \(CONST\), but it does not yet reconstruct the full collapse package. The next pressure point is the essence/necessary-existence branch:
\[
  \operatorname{Ess}
  \longrightarrow
  NE
  \longrightarrow
  A5
  \longrightarrow
  \Box\exists^E x\,G(x).
\]
