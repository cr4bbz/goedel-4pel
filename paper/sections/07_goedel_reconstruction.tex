\section{Reconstructing the G\"odel--Scott Chain}
\label{sec:reconstruction}

\statusopen

With the propositional, modal, positivity, and collapse-interface analyses fixed, the next stage is to reconstruct the remaining higher-order G\"odel--Scott machinery. The planned order is deliberately incremental:
\[
  A2
  \longrightarrow
  \text{possible exemplification}
  \longrightarrow
  A3
  \longrightarrow
  G
  \longrightarrow
  \operatorname{Ess}
  \longrightarrow
  NE
  \longrightarrow
  \Box\exists^E x\,G(x).
\]

At each stage the aim is to classify not merely whether a theorem is derivable, but which information status its conclusion may receive and which assumptions control that status. In particular, gluts \(\Both\) and gaps \(\Neither\) in positivity must not be collapsed into a single notion of failure.

Gate 4 leaves three interfaces that must now be justified rather than treated as free assumptions:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \(D1^+\), the positive-support behavior expected from Godlikeness;
  \item \(GW\), global supply of positively supported God-like witnesses;
  \item \(CONST\), embedding of arbitrary modal propositions as constant intensional properties.
\end{itemize}

The first two depend directly on the lifting of A2, A3, quantification, and the final definition of \(G\). The third is a higher-order expressivity requirement that must be checked against the selected property domain rather than silently inherited from the classical embedding.

Version 0.4 therefore leaves the final object-language definitions of Godlikeness, essence, and necessary existence open, as well as the conditional/consequence policy required to lift A2 and the definitional occurrences of implication. Gate 5 will begin with A2 and the possible-exemplification theorem before promoting any temporary interface to the final theory.
