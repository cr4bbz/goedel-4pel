\section{Reconstructing the G\"odel--Scott Chain}
\label{sec:reconstruction}

\statusopen

Only after the propositional and modal semantics are fixed will the project lift the higher-order G\"odel--Scott machinery. The planned order is deliberately incremental:
\[
  P
  \longrightarrow
  \text{possible exemplification}
  \longrightarrow
  G
  \longrightarrow
  \operatorname{Ess}
  \longrightarrow
  NE
  \longrightarrow
  \Box\exists^E x\,G(x).
\]

At each stage the aim is to classify not merely whether a theorem is derivable, but which information status its conclusion may receive and which assumptions control that status. In particular, gluts \(\Both\) and gaps \(\Neither\) in positivity must not be collapsed into a single notion of failure.

No four-valued definition of Godlikeness, essence, or necessary existence is fixed in version 0.1.
