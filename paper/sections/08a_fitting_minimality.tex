\subsection{Fitting assumption minimization}
\label{sec:fitting-minimality}

The admissible-domain reconstruction permits a second round of dependency analysis. The original recovery package
\[
  REG_{G,F}^{adm}
  =COMP_{P,F}^{G,adm}+CONS_{G,F}^{G,adm}
\]
requires every admissible extension positively possessed by a positive Fitting-Godlike witness to be locally non-glutty. The proof of the extensional essence theorem, however, uses this consistency information only when positivity completeness classifies the possessed extension negatively. I therefore isolate the weaker condition
\[
\begin{split}
  NegCons_{G,F}^{adm}:\quad
  &Adm(Y)\land G_F^+(x,w)\land \pos{Y(x)}\land \neginfo{P(Y)}@w\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow \neg\neginfo{Y(x)}.
\end{split}
\]
Writing
\[
  REG_{G,F}^{neg}
  :=COMP_{P,F}^{G,adm}+NegCons_{G,F}^{adm},
\]
Lean proves
\[
  CONS_{G,F}^{G,adm}\Longrightarrow NegCons_{G,F}^{adm}
\]
and the strengthened recovery theorem
\[
\begin{split}
  &G\text{-admissible}
  +G_F\text{-realization}
  +A1_{L,F}^{adm}
  +REG_{G,F}^{neg}\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow
  G_F^+(x,w)\Rightarrow Ess_F^+(ext_wG,x,w).
\end{split}
\]
The corresponding de-re necessary-existence chain is machine-checked with the same targeted package. A one-world one-entity finite model separates the two consistency notions on the A1-L-only route: the sole admissible extension is genuinely glutty, the targeted negative-classification condition holds, full relevant exemplification consistency fails, and the realized positive Godlike extension is nevertheless an essence.

This weakening is itself directional. If the right A1 channel is restored, Lean proves
\[
  A1_{R,F}^{adm}
  +COMP_{P,F}^{G,adm}
  +NegCons_{G,F}^{adm}
  \Longrightarrow
  CONS_{G,F}^{G,adm}.
\]
Thus the reduction is not an unconditional weakening of a theory retaining both A1 directions. It instead exposes another division of labor between the two signed A1 flows: the A1-L recovery argument needs consistency only on the negative-classification branch, while A1-R supplies enough additional complement information to reconstruct the stronger consistency condition.

\paragraph{Why completeness cannot simply be renamed away.}
Inside this classification-based proof, \(COMP_{P,F}^{G,adm}\) supplies exactly the gap branch: a relevant extension either already has positive positivity support or is negatively classified so that A1-L can activate the complement route. Replacing the disjunction by a condition of the form
\[
  \neg\pos{P(Y)}@w\Longrightarrow\neginfo{P(Y)}@w
\]
would therefore not constitute a genuine weakening at the classical meta-level; on the same relevant domain it is equivalent to the branch coverage already expressed by completeness. A real reduction of \(COMP\) must consequently change the recovery mechanism itself.

\paragraph{An alternative recovery route.}
I introduce a second, deliberately non-classificatory interface. Let admissible-property indiscernibility among positive Fitting-Godlike individuals be
\[
\begin{split}
  Ind_G^{adm}:\quad
  &Adm(Y)\land G_F^+(x,w)\land G_F^+(y,w)\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow
  \bigl(\pos{Y(x)}\leftrightarrow\pos{Y(y)}\bigr).
\end{split}
\]
This condition does not assign positive or negative positivity status to \(Y\). Instead, it constrains the selected extensional property domain: admissible rigid extensions cannot positively distinguish two positive Godlike individuals at the same source world.

Lean proves
\[
\boxed{
G\text{-admissible}
+G_F\text{-realization}
+Ind_G^{adm}
\Longrightarrow
G_F^+(x,w)\Rightarrow Ess_F^+(ext_wG,x,w).
}
\]
No A1 direction, no positivity completeness, and no exemplification-consistency premise occurs. The proof is extensional. If \(Y\) contains the selected Godlike individual \(x\), any entity \(y\) in the frozen current \(G\)-extension is also positive Godlike at the source world by \(G\)-realization. Indiscernibility then transfers positive \(Y\)-membership from \(x\) to \(y\), yielding exactly the rigid necessary entailment required by Fitting essence.

The same route continues through necessary existence. Lean machine-checks a frame-free de-re possibility-to-necessity theorem from \(G\)-admissibility, \(G\)-realization, \(Ind_G^{adm}\), NE-admissibility, NE-realization, and positive A5, with no \(COMP\), A1, or consistency premise. Positive \(G\)-extension persistence and reflection then provide the same de-dicto bridge as before.

\paragraph{Positive-profile quotient structure.}
The indiscernibility premise can itself be derived from a more structural condition on the selected property domain. Define positive-profile equivalence at a world by
\[
  x\approx_w^+ y
\]
iff every positively supported admissible extension contains \(x\) and \(y\) alike. Lean proves that \(\approx_w^+\) is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive. Positive Fitting-Godlike individuals automatically lie in the same \(\approx_w^+\)-class, because each of them positively exemplifies every positively supported admissible extension.

I then impose the quotient-style saturation condition
\[
\begin{split}
  Sat_P^{adm}:\quad
  &Adm(Y)\land x\approx_w^+y\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow
  \bigl(\pos{Y(x)}\leftrightarrow\pos{Y(y)}\bigr).
\end{split}
\]
Lean proves
\[
  Sat_P^{adm}\Longrightarrow Ind_G^{adm},
\]
and therefore the entire alternative essence/NE route follows from this property-domain condition. This gives the previously primitive indiscernibility interface a quotient-semantical interpretation rather than treating it as an isolated recovery axiom.

The quotient is in fact bilateral. Since the substantive admissible semantics is already closed under FDE negation, applying \(Sat_P^{adm}\) to \(\neg Y\) transfers constancy of positive membership into constancy of negative membership of \(Y\). Lean therefore proves
\[
\boxed{
  Sat_P^{adm}\Longleftrightarrow Sat_{\pm}^{adm},
}
\]
where \(Sat_{\pm}^{adm}\) requires both support channels of every admissible extension to be constant on the same positive-profile classes. Thus each admissible bilateral extension genuinely factors through one entity quotient rather than merely sharing a positive projection.

A one-world, three-entity finite fixture makes the result nontrivial. Two distinct entities \(a\) and \(b\) are both positive Godlike and form one non-singleton positive-profile class, while \(c\) is not Godlike. The negation-closed admissible domain consists of the rigid extensions
\[
  \varnothing,\qquad \{a,b\},\qquad \{c\},\qquad \{a,b,c\}.
\]
Every admissible extension respects the \(\{a,b\}\) profile class, so \(Sat_P^{adm}\), its bilateral counterpart, and \(Ind_G^{adm}\) hold. Nevertheless the universal extension is a genuine positivity gap:
\[
  \neg\pos{P(\{a,b,c\})}
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  \neg\neginfo{P(\{a,b,c\})},
\]
so \(COMP_{P,F}^{G,adm}\) fails at an extension containing both Godlike individuals. The current \(G\)-extension \(\{a,b\}\) is still an essence of each, and the finite NE/A5 interface remains satisfied. Hence the profile-saturation route bypasses positivity completeness non-vacuously even when Godlike indiscernibility has real two-individual content.

\paragraph{Algebraic stability of the quotient.}
The quotient interpretation is compatible with the basic FDE property operations. At the extension level define conjunction and disjunction pointwise by
\[
\begin{array}{rclcrcl}
 +(X\wedge_FY)(x)&\Leftrightarrow&+X(x)\land+Y(x),&&
 -(X\wedge_FY)(x)&\Leftrightarrow&-X(x)\lor-Y(x),\\
 +(X\vee_FY)(x)&\Leftrightarrow&+X(x)\lor+Y(x),&&
 -(X\vee_FY)(x)&\Leftrightarrow&-X(x)\land-Y(x).
\end{array}
\]
Lean proves that, for each fixed positive-profile quotient, the bilateral extensions that factor through it contain the FDE top and bottom extensions and are closed under FDE negation, conjunction, and disjunction. In this precise preservation sense the quotient-respecting extensions form a De-Morgan-style subalgebra of the full bilateral extension algebra.

This theorem does not itself impose algebraic closure on \(Adm\). It establishes the weaker and methodologically prior fact that adding FDE compounds of quotient-respecting extensions cannot break the quotient factorization. Whether the intended Fitting property domain should actually be closed under all of these operations remains a separate semantic choice.

\paragraph{Relation to Fitting's ultrafilter structure.}
The published mechanized analysis proves that Fitting's extensional positivity predicate constitutes a \(\delta\)-ultrafilter \cite{BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019}. Its classical maximality component chooses an extension or its complement as positive. To compare that architecture with the bilateral reconstruction, I isolate a four-valued complement-decision interface:
\[
  Adm(Y)\Longrightarrow
  \bigl(\pos{P(Y)}\lor\pos{P(\neg Y)}\bigr).
\]
This condition alone is not yet a four-valued ultrafilter definition. More importantly, the bilateral setting changes what the complement branch supplies: positive support for \(\neg Y\) yields negative support information about membership in \(Y\), but it does not by itself erase positive support for \(Y\).

Lean shows that complement decision combined with full relevant exemplification consistency already recovers local positive reflection at Godlike witnesses, and hence \(COMP_{P,F}^{G,adm}\). Independently, complement decision together with \(A1_R\) also yields \(COMP_{P,F}^{G,adm}\). Thus the ultrafilter-style route explains why completeness is natural at the classical Fitting boundary, but it does not account for the genuinely classification-free route above. The quotient-saturation condition and ultrafilter maximality therefore locate two different ways of controlling the property domain.

A separate minimization concerns de-dicto stability. Full bilateral extension stability
\[
  STAB_G^{\pm}:\qquad
  wRz\Rightarrow ext_wG\equiv ext_zG
\]
contains more information than the positive theorem uses. I split the positive channel into persistence and reflection:
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
  Pers_G^+&:&wRz\land \pos{ext_wG(x)}\Rightarrow\pos{ext_zG(x)},\\
  Refl_G^+&:&wRz\land \pos{ext_zG(x)}\Rightarrow\pos{ext_wG(x)}.
\end{array}
\]
Their conjunction is written \(STAB_G^+\). Lean proves that bilateral stability implies \(STAB_G^+\), but the two positive directions are exactly what the de-dicto proof consumes: reflection converts de-dicto possibility into frozen de-re possibility, whereas persistence converts frozen de-re necessity back into de-dicto necessity. Consequently the minimized classification-route theorem is
\[
\begin{split}
  &G\text{-admissible}
  +G_F\text{-realization}
  +A1_{L,F}^{adm}
  +REG_{G,F}^{neg}\\
  &+NE_F\text{-realization}
  +A5_F^+
  +STAB_G^+
  +\Diamond^+\exists^E x\,G_F(x)\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow
  \Box^+\exists^E x\,G_F(x).
\end{split}
\]
No negative-extension stability and no S4/S5 frame condition occurs. Replacing the classification block by \(Ind_G^{adm}\), or more structurally by \(Sat_P^{adm}\), gives an alternative theorem with the same positive stability interface.

The positive stability interface can now be derived from a more structural premise. Let \(R_{F,adm}^+\) restrict positive-property rigidity to the selected admissible extension domain:
\[
  Adm(X)\land\pos{P_w(X)}\land wRz
  \Longrightarrow \pos{P_z(X)}.
\]
Lean proves the asymmetric pair
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
G_F\text{-realization}+R_{F,adm}^+&\Longrightarrow&Refl_G^+,\\
G_F\text{-realization}+R_{F,adm}^++Sym(R)&\Longrightarrow&Pers_G^+.
\end{array}
\]
Forward rigidity is enough for reflection because every extension positive at the source remains positive at the target, where target Godlikeness consumes it. Persistence requires the converse transport: symmetry reverses the edge so that an extension positive at the target can be carried back to source Godlikeness. Hence
\[
  G_F\text{-realization}+R_{F,adm}^++Sym(R)
  \Longrightarrow STAB_G^+.
\]
Substituting this derived interface into the minimized theorem yields positive de-dicto necessity from \(R_{F,adm}^++Sym(R)\). The direct theorem with primitive \(STAB_G^+\) remains frame-free; symmetry re-enters only when positive \(G\)-stability is derived from forward positivity rigidity.

The weakening of stability is strict even under a stronger surrounding control theory. A complete two-world S5 fixture satisfies both A1 directions and the original full admissible regularity package. The positive membership set of \(G\) is \(\{a\}\) at both worlds, but negative information about a non-Godlike individual changes from falsity support to a gap. Hence \(STAB_G^+\) holds while \(STAB_G^{\pm}\) fails, and positive necessary de-dicto Godlikeness still holds. This finite witness shows that the positive theorem is genuinely insensitive to drift confined to the negative extension channel.

A second finite regression independently checks the derived bridge over all 256 two-world, one-entity configurations on the negation-closed classical admissible domain \(\{\True,\False\}\). Of these, 152 satisfy \(R_{F,adm}^+\), and every one satisfies \(Refl_G^+\); 80 additionally have a symmetric accessibility relation, and every one also satisfies \(Pers_G^+\). Explicit fixtures show that \(R_{F,adm}^+\) without symmetry need not force persistence and that symmetry without \(R_{F,adm}^+\) need not force reflection. These are bounded separation results, not global necessity claims.

These results do not establish global minimality. They reveal two distinct recovery architectures inside the same Fitting candidate: one resolves positivity gaps by classification, while the other factors the admissible bilateral property domain through positive-property profiles and thereby makes the gap irrelevant to extensional essence.
