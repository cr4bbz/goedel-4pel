\section{Bilateral Positivity}
\label{sec:positivity}

\statusopen

The positivity predicate \(P\) is the central non-logical primitive of the Scott theory. In the four-valued reconstruction, statements of the form \(P(\varphi)\) may themselves carry positive and negative information independently.

Two classical axioms are immediate decomposition targets. Scott's A1,
\[
  \neg P(\varphi)\leftrightarrow P(\neg\varphi),
\]
may conceal separate constraints connecting positive and negative information under property negation. Candidate components include
\[
  A1^{+}:\quad \pos{P(\neg\varphi)}\Leftrightarrow\neginfo{P(\varphi)},
\]
\[
  A1^{-}:\quad \neginfo{P(\neg\varphi)}\Leftrightarrow\pos{P(\varphi)}.
\]
Likewise A4,
\[
  P(\varphi)\rightarrow\Box P(\varphi),
\]
suggests the provisional rigidity components
\[
  \Rp:\quad \pos{P(\varphi)}\Rightarrow\pos{\Box P(\varphi)},
\]
\[
  \Rm:\quad \neginfo{P(\varphi)}\Rightarrow\neginfo{\Box P(\varphi)}.
\]
These are research candidates, not yet axioms of the final theory.

\begin{conjecture}[Gap/glut asymmetry for positivity]
Assignments \(P(\varphi)=\Both\) and \(P(\varphi)=\Neither\) propagate differently through Godlikeness and possible exemplification.
\end{conjecture}
\statusconjectural
