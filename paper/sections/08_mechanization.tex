\section{Mechanization and Countermodels}
\label{sec:mechanization}

\statusopen

Mechanization is intentionally downstream of the semantic design. Once the modal and higher-order clauses are stable, the project will formalize the settled theory in Lean and add finite-model or countermodel tooling where useful.

The minimum mechanization goals are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(M\arabic*)]
  \item machine-check the propositional kernel and its classical recovery;
  \item encode the selected modal semantics;
  \item verify each promoted bilateral axiom separately;
  \item prove positive dependency results;
  \item retain explicit finite countermodels for every claimed non-implication;
  \item recover the frozen Scott baseline on the classical fragment.
\end{enumerate}

The repository treats a minimal countermodel as a result rather than as a failed proof attempt.
