\section{Mechanization and Countermodels}
\label{sec:mechanization}

\statusopen

The semantic reconstruction is now sufficiently stable for a dedicated mechanization phase. Gate 7 should no longer treat formalization as a generic future task; it has a concrete verification target centered on the Gate-6 T2 obstruction.

The minimum mechanization goals are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(M\arabic*)]
  \item machine-check the propositional kernel, modal clauses, and classical recovery;
  \item encode the actualist quantifier semantics, \(G\text{-sup-v0.1}\), \(Ess\text{-sup-v0.1}\), and \(NE\text{-sup-v0.1}\);
  \item verify the directional A1 and rigidity results from Gates 3--4;
  \item verify \(T1_T\) and its glut countermodel;
  \item reproduce the two finite S5 countermodels to \(T2^+\), including the glut and gap variants;
  \item prove the sufficient recovery theorem
  \[
    A1_L+R^++G\text{-sup-v0.1}+REG_G
    \Rightarrow T2^+;
  \]
  \item prove the conditional positive T3 theorem and the implication \(T3^+\Rightarrow GW\);
  \item prove the essence-compressed modal-collapse theorem
  \[
    T2^++T3^++CONST\Rightarrow\MCp;
  \]
  \item verify end-to-end classical recovery of the reconstructed Scott branch;
  \item search finite models systematically for weaker substitutes for \(COMP_P^G\) and \(CONS_G^G\).
\end{enumerate}

The mechanization should distinguish two tasks. Lean is appropriate for the semantic definitions, recovery theorems, and dependency proofs. A small exhaustive finite-model checker may be more efficient for countermodel minimization and assumption-lattice search. Results should be cross-checked where feasible rather than relying on a single implementation path.

The repository treats a minimal countermodel as a result rather than as a failed proof attempt. In particular, the Gate-6 glut and gap models should become regression fixtures: any later change of semantics that makes them disappear must identify exactly which new assumption excludes them.
