\section{Mechanization and Countermodels}
\label{sec:mechanization}

\statusestablished

Gate 7 adds two complementary verification layers. I use a finite-model oracle for concrete countermodels and bounded exhaustive searches, and Lean for general semantic theorems that must not depend on a finite bound. Gate 8 extends the same discipline to comparative variants and frame minimization. This separation prevents the absence of a small countermodel from being mistaken for a proof.

\subsection{Finite-model regression layer}

The executable checker implements the four information values, involutive FDE negation, finite accessibility relations, actualist existence, four-valued property extensions and positivity, signed necessary entailment, support-based Godlikeness, positive essence, and the Gate-6 regularity conditions.

The regression suite machine-checks both two-world T2 countermodels from Gate 6. The glut model satisfies the encoded Gate-5 control stack and \(COMP_P^G\) but violates \(CONS_G^G\), while the gap model satisfies the same control stack and \(CONS_G^G\) but violates \(COMP_P^G\). Both refute \(T2^+\).

The checker also validates the Gate-5 obstruction to unrestricted positive T1. A one-world reflexive model with no actual entities and glutty positivity for a property and its complement satisfies strong A1 and \(A2^+\), while positive support for the property does not yield positive possible actual exemplification. This is the executable counterpart of the distinction between mere positive support and truth-only positivity.

A first bounded search retains 204 models satisfying the original small control antecedent and verifies \(T2^+\) in every retained model. A broader exhaustive two-world, one-entity \(G,Z\) family then varies positivity of \(Z\) and its complement independently while fixing FDE complement exemplification and full bilateral \(G\)-support semantics. In this broader family, 873 models satisfy
\[
 A1_L,\qquad R^+,\qquad COMP_P^G,\qquad CONS_G^G,
\]
and every one of them satisfies \(T2^+\).

More importantly, dropping any one of these four assumptions while retaining the other three produces a \(T2^+\) countermodel within the same bounded family. Thus the search establishes bounded individual indispensability of the four recovery assumptions relative to the fixed semantic laws. I do not promote this result to global model-theoretic minimality.

The checker also verifies the schema-level equivalence
\[
  \MCp\leftrightarrow\MCm
\]
on a negation-closed two-world test family. This regression records an important typing distinction: the equivalence is a result about schemata closed under substitution by negation, not a pointwise identity for one isolated formula valuation.

Gate 8 adds three further families of executable witnesses. First, the support/exact comparison confirms that both Gate-6 T2 countermodels are support-Godlike but fail project-internal exact positive Godlikeness, while a separate exact-Godlike fixture still permits genuine \(\Both\) gluts. Second, a complete-S5 bilateral Anderson fixture has positive necessary Godlike existence while a contingent formula application refutes positive modal collapse. Third, both the Scott-support and Anderson branches have S4-style two-world countermodels: the frames are reflexive and transitive but not symmetric, satisfy the remaining encoded premises of the reduced T3 routes, and refute the corresponding positive T3 conclusion. These are explicit countermodels to replacing symmetry by S4 in the current routes; they are not global minimality proofs.

\subsection{Lean layer}

The Lean package is pinned to Lean 4.30.0 and uses a declarative Lake TOML configuration. It formalizes the four-valued carrier, FDE negation, conjunction and disjunction, bilateral relational modality, bilateral actualist quantification, signed necessary entailment, support-based Godlikeness, bilateral essence, and the positive necessary-existence interface.

Lean proves the collapse-channel theorem generally at schema level. It also proves the Gate-5 truth-only possibility theorem
\[
  A1_R+A2^+\Longrightarrow T1_T,
\]
using classical reductio only at the semantic metalevel.

The principal Gate-6 recovery theorem is machine-checked without a finite bound:
\[
  NegExemplification
  +G\text{-sup}
  +A1_L
  +R^+
  +REG_G
  \Longrightarrow T2^+.
\]
The proof therefore confirms that no additional Scott axiom is hidden in the paper derivation.

Gate 8 sharpens the later modal dependency. Once Scott \(T2^+\) is available, Lean proves
\[
  \operatorname{Sym}(R)
  +PossibleGod
  +T2^+
  +A5^+
  +NE\text{-sup}
  +G\text{-sup}
  \Longrightarrow T3^+.
\]
Reflexivity and transitivity are absent from this theorem. Reflexivity is required only for the separate implication \(T3^+\Rightarrow GW\) and for the current essence-compressed collapse theorem, which Lean proves as
\[
  \operatorname{Refl}(R)
  +T2^+
  +T3^+
  +G\text{-sup}
  +CONST
  \Longrightarrow\MCp.
\]

The comparative branch is also mechanized. For project-internal exact positive Godlikeness, Lean proves
\[
  G\text{-exact}^+\Rightarrow G\text{-sup}^+
\]
and
\[
  G\text{-exact}^+ + R^+\Longrightarrow T2_{\mathrm{exact}}^+.
\]
The latter theorem has no \(A1_L\), \(COMP_P^G\), or \(CONS_G^G\) premise because local positive reflection is built into exactness.

For the Anderson comparison I first encode the literature-grounded positive interfaces using directional metalanguage clauses rather than a four-valued object-language biconditional. A project-specific bilateral negative-evidence reconstruction then adds explicit mismatch witnesses. Under classical coherence Lean proves that negative Anderson necessary exemplification, Godlikeness, essence, necessary actual exemplification, and necessary existence are exactly the complements of their positive counterparts.

The positive Anderson necessary-existence branch is also machine-checked. Its frame assumption can be reduced to symmetry alone:
\[
\begin{split}
  &\operatorname{Sym}(R)
  +PossibleG_A
  +G_A\text{-realization}
  +P^+(G_A)
  +A2^+\\
  &\qquad
  +NE_A\text{-realization}
  +P^+(NE_A)
  \Longrightarrow T3_A^+.
\end{split}
\]
No reflexivity, transitivity, or separate \(R^+\) premise occurs in that theorem.

The Fitting comparison now goes beyond the type boundary. Lean distinguishes bilateral property extensions from intensions, represents an intension as a world-indexed extension, provides explicit \(extensionAt\) and rigidification operations, and types Fitting positivity over extensions rather than intensions. Over that typed interface I define signed necessary entailment between rigid extensions, bilateral extensional essence, and bilateral necessary existence.

The first general Fitting theorem is
\[
\begin{split}
  &G_F\text{-realization}
  +A1_{L,F}
  +REG_G^{\mathrm{ext}}\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow
  \bigl(G_F^+(x,w)\Rightarrow
  Ess_F^+(\operatorname{ext}_w G,x,w)\bigr).
\end{split}
\]
Unlike the verified Scott-support T2 recovery theorem, this result has no \(R^+\) premise. The reason is structural rather than syntactic: Fitting entailment compares rigid extensions, so after local positive reflection has recovered \(P(Y)\) at \(w\), membership in the frozen current \(G\)-extension is evaluated against the same extensional \(Y\) at every accessible world. No transport of positivity to that accessible world is required.

With an intensional \(NE\) realizing the extension-typed necessary-existence condition and positive A5 applied to its current extension, Lean further proves a de-re possibility-to-necessity theorem:
\[
\begin{split}
  &G_F\text{-realization}
  +A1_{L,F}
  +REG_G^{\mathrm{ext}}
  +NE_F\text{-realization}
  +A5_F^+\\
  &\qquad
  +\Diamond_{\mathrm{de\,re}}^+\exists^E x\,\operatorname{ext}_wG(x)
  \Longrightarrow
  \Box_{\mathrm{de\,re}}^+\exists^E x\,\operatorname{ext}_wG(x).
\end{split}
\]
No reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, seriality, or positivity-rigidity premise occurs. The extension of \(G\) is frozen at the evaluation world throughout this theorem. I do not identify this with the de-dicto claim in which \(G\) is re-evaluated at each accessible world; the published Fitting formalization itself distinguishes these readings and reports non-equivalences between tempting formulations \cite{BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019}.

\subsection{Classical recovery}

I also formalize the recovery boundary explicitly. Under classical coherence of the positive and negative exemplification channels, Lean proves that negative support for necessary entailment is exactly failure of positive necessary entailment, and that the negative Godlikeness, essence, and necessary-existence clauses are the complements of their positive clauses. With classical coherence of positivity, truth-only positivity reduces to ordinary positive positivity support. Thus the bilateral D1/D2/D3 interfaces collapse to ordinary bivalent behavior on the classical fragment.

The Anderson bilateral candidate has an analogous machine-checked recovery layer. This is important methodologically: its negative clauses are a project reconstruction, so their classical limit is tested rather than assumed. The corresponding full Fitting recovery audit remains open at this version; the new Fitting positive theorems are therefore stated only for the explicitly defined signed interfaces.

These are interface-level recovery results rather than a claim that the entire AFP/HOL developments have been reimplemented line by line. Exact higher-order correspondence with the frozen Scott baseline and published Anderson/Fitting formalizations remains a publication audit item.

\subsection{Verification discipline}

The repository treats a minimal countermodel as a result rather than as a failed proof attempt. Conversely, every exhaustive finite search is reported with its exact model bound. General claims are promoted to machine-checked status only after an unbounded Lean theorem is present.

The executable and Lean sources are maintained under \texttt{formal/}, with CI configured to run both tracks on repository updates. Gate 7 closes the first verification phase; Gate 8 now uses the same machinery to compare definitions, expose modal-frame dependencies, and isolate the intension/extension choices that distinguish literature variants.
