\section{Mechanization and Countermodels}
\label{sec:mechanization}

\statusopen

Gate 7 has begun with two complementary verification layers. A finite-model oracle records concrete countermodels and bounded exhaustive searches, while Lean is reserved for general semantic theorems that should not depend on a finite bound. This separation prevents the absence of a small countermodel from being mistaken for a proof.

\subsection{Finite-model regression layer}

The executable checker currently implements the four information values, involutive FDE negation, finite S5 accessibility, actualist existence, four-valued property extensions and positivity, semantic necessary entailment, support-based Godlikeness, positive essence, and the Gate-6 regularity conditions.

The first regression suite machine-checks both two-world T2 countermodels from Gate 6. The glut model satisfies the encoded Gate-5 control stack and \(COMP_P^G\) but violates \(CONS_G^G\), while the gap model satisfies the same control stack and \(CONS_G^G\) but violates \(COMP_P^G\). Both refute \(T2^+\).

A bounded exhaustive search over the complete currently generated two-world, one-entity \(G,Z\) fragment retains 204 models satisfying strong A1, positive rigidity, support-based Godlikeness, and \(REG_G\). Every retained model satisfies \(T2^+\). This is a regression result for that explicitly bounded model class, not a substitute for the general semantic proof.

The checker also exposed a useful typing distinction in the collapse result. The statement
\[
  \MCp\leftrightarrow\MCm
\]
is an equivalence of schemata closed under substitution by negation; it is not a pointwise identity for one isolated formula valuation. The finite regression therefore carries a negation-closed formula family and checks all 16 two-world assignments to its generator.

\subsection{Lean layer}

The Lean package is pinned to Lean 4.30.0. Its first modules formalize the four-valued carrier and FDE negation, including involution and the \(\True/\False\) swap with fixed points \(\Both\) and \(\Neither\). A separate analysis module states positive modal collapse and universal negative persistence as schemata over arbitrary worlds and formulas and proves their equivalence from bilateral negation and involution.

The next Lean targets are the relational bilateral modal clauses, actualist quantification, semantic necessary entailment, support-based Godlikeness, and positive essence. The principal theorem target is a general formal proof of the Gate-6 implication
\[
  A1_L+R^++G\text{-sup}+REG_G\Longrightarrow T2^+,
\]
followed by the positive T3, witness-supply, and essence-compressed collapse theorems.

\subsection{Verification discipline}

The repository treats a minimal countermodel as a result rather than as a failed proof attempt. Conversely, every exhaustive finite search is reported with its exact model bound. General claims are promoted to machine-checked status only after a proof-assistant theorem or an equivalent unbounded verification argument is present.

The executable and Lean sources are maintained under \texttt{formal/}, with CI configured to run both tracks on repository updates.
