\section{Conclusion and Research Agenda}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This manuscript begins from a deliberately narrow claim. The G\"odel--Scott theory is not merely an object to be translated from two values to four. It is a controlled test case for asking where classically unitary modal principles conceal distinct informational dependencies.

At version 0.4 six pieces are in place. First, the Scott theory is frozen as a classical control system. Second, the propositional semantics is a conservative Belnap--Dunn/FDE kernel that is paraconsistent, paracomplete, and classically recoverable. Third, the classical modal-collapse proof has been reduced to a dependency spine. Fourth, the modal layer is fixed by a bilateral relational semantics with classical recovery and an exact paired-neighborhood representation. Fifth, the positivity layer decomposes A1 into two independent directional information flows and distinguishes positive from universal-negative positivity rigidity. Sixth, Gate 4 determines where that decomposition survives and where it does not.

The first Gate-4 result falsifies the original bilateral-collapse separation conjecture for the present semantics. The informative schemata
\[
  \MCp:\quad \pos{\chi}\Rightarrow\pos{\Box\chi}
\]
and
\[
  \MCm:\quad \neginfo{\chi}\Rightarrow\pos{\Box\neg\chi}
\]
are equivalent because FDE negation is involutive and the schemata range over all formulas. Negative collapse is simply positive collapse instantiated at \(\neg\chi\), and conversely.

The informative decomposition therefore occurs upstream. Without A1, positive and universal-negative positivity rigidity are independent, as shown by two-world S5 countermodels. Under strong A1 they become interderivable. More importantly, the classical local reflection step
\[
  G(x),Z(x)\Rightarrow P(Z)
\]
requires additional informational regularity in the four-valued setting. At the current abstract interface, the package
\[
  A1_L+D1^++CONS_G+COMP_P
\]
is sufficient for positive reflection and locally minimal relative to that vocabulary: gluts witness the need for exemplification consistency, while gaps witness the need for positivity completeness.

Adding positive rigidity, a global supply of positively supported God-like witnesses, and constant-property embedding yields an interface-level derivation of modal collapse. This makes the next research question substantially sharper than the original one. Gate 5 must determine whether the full four-valued reconstruction of A2, A3, D1, and higher-order quantification actually supplies, weakens, or replaces those interfaces. In particular, the project must not promote \(CONS_G\), \(COMP_P\), \(GW\), or \(CONST\) to axioms merely because they recover the classical proof.

The emerging thesis is therefore one of \emph{upstream decomposition}: symmetric FDE negation recouples the final collapse schemata, while the substantive four-valued variation resides in positivity-negation transfer, rigidity, reflection, and the higher-order infrastructure connecting local properties to arbitrary modal propositions.

The paper will continue to be revised gate by gate. Open sections remain explicit records of claims the current theory has not yet earned.
