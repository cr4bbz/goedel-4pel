\section{Conclusion and Research Agenda}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This manuscript begins from a deliberately narrow claim. The G\"odel--Scott theory is not merely an object to be translated from two values to four. It is a controlled test case for asking where classically unitary modal principles conceal distinct informational dependencies.

At version 0.5 the reconstruction reaches the first higher-order existence branch. Gates 1--4 establish the bilateral propositional and modal semantics, decompose A1 and A4, falsify the original collapse-separation conjecture under involutive FDE negation, and isolate a locally minimal package recovering the classical reflection step from God-like exemplification to positivity.

Gate 5 adds a second kind of upstream decomposition. The classical theorem that positive properties are possibly exemplified does not survive unchanged when positive support may be glutty. Rather, with semantic necessary positive entailment \(A2^+\) and the right-to-left informational direction \(A1_R\), the recovered theorem is
\[
  \operatorname{Pos}_{T}(\varphi)
  \Rightarrow
  \pos{\Diamond\exists^E x\,\varphi(x)},
\]
where \(\operatorname{Pos}_{T}\) means positive support without simultaneous negative support. A one-world model with an empty actual domain and glutty positivity shows why the weaker antecedent \(\pos{P(\varphi)}\) is insufficient: the classical reductio merely generates negative positivity support already present in the glut.

This result permits a Scott-control reading of A3,
\[
  A3_T:\quad \operatorname{Pos}_{T}(G),
\]
from which positive possible God-like existence follows. At the same time, support-based Godlikeness can be defined bilaterally so that every positively supported positive property generates a positive exemplification obligation, while a negatively exemplified positive property witnesses negative Godlikeness. The resulting definition recovers Scott D1 on the classical fragment and permits all four information states outside it.

The Gate-4 collapse audit therefore becomes more precise. Two former placeholders are now justified: \(D1^+\) follows from support-based Godlikeness, and constant-property embedding follows from the retained higher-order lambda-abstraction/comprehension setting. Three crucial interfaces remain unforced. Possible God-like existence does not yet supply the global witness condition \(GW\); strong A1 does not eliminate \(N/N\) gaps and hence does not supply \(COMP_P\); and support-based Godlikeness does not guarantee consistency of arbitrary exemplified properties, so \(CONS_G\) remains independent.

The next research question is consequently concentrated in Scott's essence and necessary-existence branch. Gate 6 must determine whether natural four-valued liftings of D2, D3, and A5 can recover an analogue of T3 strong enough to discharge \(GW\), and whether doing so introduces new glut/gap obstructions or unexpectedly supplies principled replacements for \(CONS_G\) or \(COMP_P\).

The emerging thesis remains one of \emph{upstream decomposition}: symmetric FDE negation recouples the final collapse schemata, while the substantive four-valued variation resides in positivity-negation transfer, rigidity, possible exemplification, reflection, and the higher-order witness structure that connects these local mechanisms to modal collapse.

The paper will continue to be revised gate by gate. Open sections remain explicit records of claims the current theory has not yet earned.
