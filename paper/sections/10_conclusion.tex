\section{Conclusion and Research Agenda}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This manuscript treats the G\"odel--Scott theory not merely as an object to be translated from two values to four, but as a controlled test case for locating informational dependencies that classical bivalence suppresses.

By version 0.19 the Scott reconstruction and verification branches reach positive necessary Godlike existence, the associated collapse analysis, a mechanically supported comparison of three different Godlikeness/essence architectures, and an exact paired-neighborhood representation of the relational modal control. Gates 1--4 establish the bilateral propositional and modal semantics, decompose A1 and A4, falsify the original collapse-separation conjecture under involutive FDE negation, and isolate glut/gap failures in the classical reflection step. Gate 5 reconstructs truth-only positive-property exemplification, a control reading of A3, possible positive Godlike existence, and support-based bilateral Godlikeness.

Gate 6 identifies the sharper Scott bottleneck. A natural bilateral definition of essence recovers Scott D2 classically, but it does not make Godlikeness an essence automatically. Two explicit two-world S5 models satisfy the Gate-5 control conditions together with strong A1 and positive rigidity while refuting
\[
  T2^+:\quad
  \pos{G(x)}\Rightarrow\pos{G\operatorname{Ess}x}.
\]
The glut and gap models fail for different informational reasons. A targeted regularity package recovers T2, and Gate 7 changes the epistemic status of this result by machine-checking
\[
  NegExemplification
  +G\text{-sup}
  +A1_L
  +R^+
  +REG_G
  \Longrightarrow T2^+.
\]
Lean also verifies the earlier truth-only possibility theorem
\[
  A1_R+A2^+\Longrightarrow T1_T.
\]
Thus the division of labor inside Scott's A1 is represented in independent formal derivations.

Once T2 is available, the remaining Scott necessary-existence branch requires less frame structure than the original S5 presentation suggested. Gate 8 proves
\[
\begin{split}
  &\operatorname{Sym}(R)
  +PossibleGod
  +T2^+
  +A5^+
  +NE\text{-sup}
  +G\text{-sup}\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow T3^+.
\end{split}
\]
Neither reflexivity nor transitivity is a premise. A reflexive-transitive but non-symmetric finite model refutes the corresponding S4 replacement. Reflexivity reappears later, where T3 supplies the global witness condition and where the current essence-compressed positive collapse theorem uses
\[
  \operatorname{Refl}(R)+T2^++T3^++G\text{-sup}+CONST
  \Longrightarrow\MCp.
\]
The informative negative collapse schema then follows from the independently machine-checked collapse-channel equivalence.

The finite-model layer provides complementary negative evidence. The broader exhaustive two-world, one-entity \(G,Z\) family retains 873 models satisfying the full current Scott T2 recovery package, and every retained model satisfies \(T2^+\). Dropping any one of \(A1_L\), positive rigidity, relevant positivity completeness, or relevant exemplification consistency yields a T2 countermodel in the same bounded family. The first \(A1_L\)-drop witness is now rendered directly from the oracle as a two-node Kripke graph with complete local FDE tables, making both its accessibility relation and its positive/negative support matrix independently inspectable. This remains bounded indispensability, not global necessity.

Gate 8 demonstrates that the T2 bottleneck is definition-sensitive. The project-internal exact positive Godlikeness condition internalizes local positive reflection. Lean consequently proves
\[
  G\text{-exact}^+ + R^+\Longrightarrow T2^+_{\mathrm{exact}}
\]
without \(A1_L\), \(COMP_P^G\), or \(CONS_G^G\), while an executable model shows that exact positive Godlikeness remains compatible with genuine gluts.

The Anderson comparison shifts the modal work differently. Its literature-grounded positive interface classifies necessary properties of an individual through positivity and modifies essence accordingly. The project adds bilateral negative-evidence clauses and machine-checks their classical recovery. A complete-S5 model of the candidate has positive necessary Godlike existence while positive modal collapse fails for a contingent formula application. The Anderson T3 branch also reduces to symmetry alone, with a corresponding non-symmetric S4 countermodel. Scott and Anderson therefore share the same currently verified T3 return bridge while differing substantially in the upstream Godlikeness/essence mechanism.

The Fitting comparison exposes a third and more radical axis: the location of the property domain itself. The mechanized literature distinguishes intensions from extensions and applies Fitting positivity to extensions \cite{BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019}. Enforcing that distinction in the four-valued setting first produced an instructive failure. If the extensional domain is identified with every bilateral support assignment, it contains a universal glut extension. Lean proves that the resulting unrestricted relevant-consistency condition excludes every positive Fitting-Godlike individual. The first unrestricted recovery implication was therefore logically correct but unsuitable as a substantive non-vacuous theorem.

The repaired candidate introduces a selected domain \(Adm\) of admissible extensions, closed under FDE negation but not globally constrained to consistency. On this domain Lean proves the non-vacuous Fitting essence theorem
\[
\begin{split}
  &G\text{-admissible}
  +G_F\text{-realization}
  +A1_{L,F}^{adm}
  +REG_{G,F}^{adm}\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow
  G_F^+(x,w)\Rightarrow Ess_F^+(ext_wG,x,w),
\end{split}
\]
with no positivity-rigidity premise. This is a genuine structural difference from the current Scott-support recovery route: extensional necessary entailment operates on rigid extensions, so positivity need not be transported to each accessible world.

Gate 8 then minimizes this Fitting route further. Full relevant exemplification consistency is stronger than the A1-L proof itself requires: it is sufficient to demand consistency only when positivity completeness classifies the currently possessed extension negatively. Writing this targeted condition as \(NegCons_{G,F}^{adm}\), Lean proves
\[
\begin{split}
  &G\text{-admissible}
  +G_F\text{-realization}
  +A1_{L,F}^{adm}\\
  &\qquad
  +COMP_{P,F}^{G,adm}
  +NegCons_{G,F}^{adm}\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow
  G_F^+(x,w)\Rightarrow Ess_F^+(ext_wG,x,w).
\end{split}
\]
A finite glut model separates the targeted condition from full consistency on the A1-L-only route. The weakening is itself A1-sensitive: restoring \(A1_R\) allows Lean to reconstruct full relevant exemplification consistency from positivity completeness plus \(NegCons_{G,F}^{adm}\). Thus the two A1 channels again have distinct structural roles even inside the Fitting comparison.

A second Fitting recovery route shows that positivity completeness is itself route-specific rather than globally necessary for the current essence/NE conclusion. The previously isolated Godlike-indiscernibility interface is now derived from a quotient-style condition on \(Adm\). Let
\[
  x\approx_w^+y
\]
mean that every positively supported admissible extension contains \(x\) and \(y\) alike. Lean proves this to be an equivalence relation and proves that positive Fitting-Godlike individuals necessarily lie in the same equivalence class. If every admissible rigid extension is saturated with respect to these positive-profile classes, then
\[
  Sat_P^{adm}\Longrightarrow Ind_G^{adm},
\]
and therefore
\[
  G\text{-admissible}
  +G_F\text{-realization}
  +Sat_P^{adm}
  \Longrightarrow
  G_F^+(x,w)\Rightarrow Ess_F^+(ext_wG,x,w)
\]
without A1, \(COMP_P^{G,adm}\), or exemplification consistency. A one-world three-entity finite model contains two distinct positive Godlike individuals in one non-singleton profile class, satisfies the saturation condition, and still has a relevant admissible extension with positivity value \(\Neither\). Thus the classification gap remains real while the essence/NE route succeeds.

The quotient condition has a more canonical formulation than the initial indiscernibility interface suggested. FDE-negation closure upgrades positive-profile saturation to bilateral factorization, and quotient-respecting bilateral extensions are closed under the basic FDE operations. Ordinary algebra closure does not generate the quotient: a complete classical extension algebra can be closed under negation, conjunction, and disjunction while still containing a singleton that distinguishes two positivity-profile-equivalent individuals.

The entailment experiment sharpens this boundary further. The classical \(\delta\)-filter closure described in the literature is closure of the family of positive extensions, not closure of the ambient property domain \cite{BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019}. Naively imposing global entailment closure on \(Adm\) is too strong once FDE bottom is admissible, since bottom positively entails every extension. Lean proves
\[
  EntCl^{adm}+Adm(\bot_F)
  \Longrightarrow
  \forall Y\,Adm(Y),
\]
and, with relevant exemplification consistency, this recreates the unrestricted-comprehension obstruction and excludes every positive Godlike witness. A separate exhaustive finite fixture over all sixteen bilateral extensions on two entities satisfies \(A2_F^+\), full domain-level entailment closure, FDE-algebra closure, and profile-compatible actual existence while profile saturation still fails. Thus entailment closure is not the missing source of the quotient.

Instead, Lean defines a canonical bilateral closure \(Sat_w\) that sends an arbitrary extension to the union of the positive-profile classes meeting its positive and negative support. This operation is extensive and monotone, idempotent up to bilateral extensional equivalence, and has the universal property of being the least quotient-respecting extension above its input. The earlier saturation principle is exactly the fixed-point condition
\[
  Sat_P^{adm}
  \Longleftrightarrow
  \forall w,Y\,(Adm(Y)\rightarrow Sat_w(Y)\equiv Y).
\]
Thus the selected Fitting property domain can be understood as living inside the fixed-point algebra of a canonical observational closure operator.

Actualist entailment introduces one additional interface. If actual existence is constant on positive-profile classes throughout the accessible region, then Lean proves
\[
  Entail_F^+(w,X,Y)
  \Longrightarrow
  Entail_F^+(w,Sat_w(X),Sat_w(Y)).
\]
A two-world finite countermodel shows that this descent can fail when two profile-equivalent individuals are split by the actual-existence predicate. The quotient analysis therefore reaches beyond property membership: actualist domain variation must also be coordinated with the entity quotient if Fitting entailment is to factor through it directly.

The quotient is now explicit in Lean. Entities at a source world are quotiented by positive admissible-property profile, and bilateral extensions are transported in both directions between quotient classes and profile-respecting entity extensions with round-trip equivalences. Moreover,
\[
  ExSat_P
  \Longleftrightarrow
  \text{actual existence factors through every accessible source quotient}.
\]
Under this exact representative-independence condition, quotient-level actualist entailment is equivalent in both directions to ordinary entailment on pullbacks. The bounded two-entity regression checks all 256 ordered pairs of saturated extensions and retains both \(\Both\) and \(\Neither\), so the construction is not a hidden return to bivalence.

The first filter analysis on this quotient gives a similarly sharp separation. In the FDE truth order, Lean defines proper delta-filters and their prime and non-adjunctive two-filter strengthenings. Point evaluation is a prime delta-filter: it designates \(\Both\), but \(\Neither\) is a self-negating gap outside the filter. Therefore primeness does not imply complement decision. The exhaustive two-point quotient audit finds four prime ordinary filters and all four are non-deciding; a separate ordinary-filter fixture has two positive-Godlike points while local \(COMP\) fails for a possessed gap-bearing extension. The remaining question is which stronger filter interface, if any, contributes to essence/necessary existence without erasing this separation.

The literature's classical Fitting analysis supplies a useful contrast. Fitting positivity is a \(\delta\)-ultrafilter on rigid extensions \cite{BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019}. Isolating only its maximality component gives a complement-decision principle. In the bilateral reconstruction, Lean shows that complement decision together with relevant exemplification consistency already reconstructs local positive reflection and hence \(COMP_P^{G,adm}\); complement decision together with \(A1_R\) also reconstructs \(COMP_P^{G,adm}\). The ultrafilter-style route therefore explains why classification is natural in the classical theory, while positive-profile saturation gives a genuinely different four-valued route that leaves relevant positivity gaps unresolved.

The admissible bilateral negative clauses also recover classically. Lean proves complementary recovery for extensional entailment, Godlikeness, essence, necessary actual exemplification, and necessary existence under classical coherence of the selected extensions. Thus the repair is not merely a positive-fragment construction.

The Fitting necessary-existence branch separates de re from de dicto explicitly. Freezing the current \(G\)-extension yields a frame-free theorem:
\[
  \text{admissible Fitting stack}
  +\Diamond_{de\,re}^+\exists^E x\,ext_wG(x)
  \Longrightarrow
  \Box_{de\,re}^+\exists^E x\,ext_wG(x).
\]
No reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, seriality, or \(R^+\) premise is used.

The earlier de-dicto bridge used full bilateral extension stability. The minimized proof reveals that only positive membership in \(G\) matters. Let \(Pers_G^+\) preserve positive \(G\)-membership from the source of an accessibility edge to its target and let \(Refl_G^+\) reflect positive membership back from target to source. Their conjunction, \(STAB_G^+\), suffices for
\[
\begin{split}
  &\text{minimized admissible Fitting stack}
  +STAB_G^+
  +\Diamond^+\exists^E x\,G_F(x)\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow
  \Box^+\exists^E x\,G_F(x),
\end{split}
\]
again without an S4 or S5 frame premise. A complete-S5 finite fixture satisfies both A1 directions and even the older full regularity package while the positive \(G\)-extension is stable and the negative \(G\)-extension drifts. Positive necessary de-dicto Godlikeness still holds. Hence full bilateral \(STAB_G\) is genuinely stronger than the positive theorem needs.

The positive stability assumption itself now has a derived form. Restrict forward positivity rigidity to admissible Fitting extensions as \(R_{F,adm}^+\), and write \(R_{F,adm}^{+\leftarrow}\) for converse transport of target positivity back to the source along an existing edge. Lean proves that the former derives \(Refl_G^+\), while the latter derives \(Pers_G^+\), so their conjunction yields all of \(STAB_G^+\) without a frame condition. Symmetry plus \(R_{F,adm}^+\) entails \(R_{F,adm}^{+\leftarrow}\), recovering the older route. A complete 256-case finite regression finds 112 candidates satisfying both transports, including 32 asymmetric candidates. Thus accessibility symmetry is strictly stronger than the bidirectional positivity transport consumed by the proof in this bounded family.

Finally, the admissible Fitting candidate admits the same qualitative anti-collapse phenomenon as the Anderson candidate. A complete two-world S5 model satisfies the encoded Fitting stack and positive necessary actual Godlikeness while a contingent \(Q(a)\) refutes \(\MCp\). The admissible property domain in that model still contains genuine \(\Both\) information, so the result is not obtained by silently restoring global bivalence.

The comparative picture is therefore sharper than a hierarchy of stronger or weaker modal logics. Scott, Anderson, and Fitting distribute the decisive structure differently:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{Scott support:} & \text{reflection recovery and }R^+\text{ precede }T2^+,\\
\text{Anderson:} & \text{necessary exemplification is built into Godlikeness},\\
\text{Fitting:} & \text{positivity/essence range over selected rigid extensions,}\\
& \text{with classification and quotient-fixed-point recovery routes.}
\end{array}
\]
The accessibility relation matters, but it is not the only place where modal strength enters the theory.

The main open questions now separate accordingly. For Scott:
\[
  \boxed{
  \begin{gathered}
  \text{Which weaker principled four-valued assumptions, if any,}\\
  \text{suffice for }T2^+\text{ globally?}
  \end{gathered}
  }
\]
For Fitting's quotient semantics:
\[
  \boxed{
  \begin{gathered}
  \text{Which four-valued filter principles on quotient fixed points}\\
  \text{preserve gaps and gluts without reconstructing }COMP\text{?}
  \end{gathered}
  }
\]
For the ultrafilter comparison:
\[
  \boxed{
  \begin{gathered}
  \text{Can a genuinely four-valued }\delta\text{-filter/ultrafilter be defined}\\
  \text{on the closure fixed points without restoring }COMP\text{?}
  \end{gathered}
  }
\]
For the remaining de-dicto minimization:
\[
  \boxed{
  \begin{gathered}
  \text{Can }R_{F,adm}^{+\leftarrow}\text{ be restricted to G-triggered}\\
  \text{or quotient-selected admissible extensions?}
  \end{gathered}
  }
\]

The modal generalization now has a precise first boundary. Every relational control model embeds exactly into the paired-neighborhood semantics, and complement duality suffices for classical modal recovery. In the complete two-world local space there are twelve non-principal complement-dual pairs beyond the four principal relational pairs, while dropping complement duality permits modal \(\Both\) and \(\Neither\) even for classical inputs. This establishes a genuine non-Kripke experimental region without yet claiming that the higher-order Scott, Anderson, or Fitting theorems transport unchanged.

Exact higher-order correspondence with the complete published formal developments, selected theorem-specific paired-neighborhood transports, and the remaining line-by-line source audit remain publication-stage requirements. The Gate-12 synthesis records this boundary explicitly. The paper continues to distinguish general Lean theorems, explicit finite countermodels, bounded exhaustive evidence, and open global-minimality claims.
