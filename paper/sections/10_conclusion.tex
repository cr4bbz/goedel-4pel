\section{Conclusion and Research Agenda}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This manuscript treats the G\"odel--Scott theory not merely as an object to be translated from two values to four, but as a controlled test case for locating informational dependencies that classical bivalence suppresses.

By version 0.8 the main Scott reconstruction and verification branches have reached positive necessary God-like existence, the associated collapse analysis, and a first comparative analysis of alternative Godlikeness/essence architectures. Gates 1--4 establish the bilateral propositional and modal semantics, decompose A1 and A4, falsify the original collapse-separation conjecture under involutive FDE negation, and isolate glut/gap failures in the classical reflection step. Gate 5 reconstructs truth-only positive-property exemplification, a control reading of A3, possible positive God-like existence, and support-based bilateral Godlikeness.

Gate 6 identifies the sharper bottleneck. A natural bilateral definition of essence recovers Scott D2 classically, but it does not make Godlikeness an essence automatically. Two explicit two-world S5 models satisfy the Gate-5 control conditions together with strong A1 and positive rigidity while refuting
\[
  T2^+:\quad
  \pos{G(x)}\Rightarrow\pos{G\operatorname{Ess}x}.
\]
The models fail for different reasons. In the glut model a locally possessed property is also negatively supported and need not be positively shared by another God-like witness. In the gap model positivity supplies no information capable of turning local possession into necessary sharing.

A targeted regularity package recovers T2. For properties positively exemplified by positive God-like witnesses, relevant positivity completeness blocks the gap route while relevant exemplification consistency blocks the glut route. Together with \(A1_L\), positive rigidity, support-based Godlikeness, and the bilateral negation law these conditions yield positive Godlikeness-as-essence.

Gate 7 changes the epistemic status of this result. Lean 4.30.0 machine-checks the general implication
\[
  NegExemplification
  +G\text{-sup}
  +A1_L
  +R^+
  +REG_G
  \Longrightarrow T2^+.
\]
The earlier truth-only possibility theorem is also machine-checked:
\[
  A1_R+A2^+\Longrightarrow T1_T.
\]
Thus the division of labor inside Scott's A1 is not only semantically motivated but represented in independent formal derivations.

Once T2 is available, the remaining necessary-existence branch is more robust than the original S5 presentation suggested. Gate 8 proves in Lean that symmetry alone is sufficient for the Scott T3 step:
\[
\begin{split}
  &\operatorname{Sym}(R)
  +PossibleGod
  +T2^+
  +A5^+
  +NE\text{-sup}
  +G\text{-sup}\\
  &\qquad\Longrightarrow T3^+.
\end{split}
\]
Neither reflexivity nor transitivity is a premise. A two-world S4-style finite countermodel is reflexive and transitive but non-symmetric, satisfies the remaining encoded premises, and refutes \(T3^+\). The result establishes symmetry as a sufficient bridge and shows that S4 alone does not replace it; it does not establish a unique globally weakest frame condition.

Reflexivity reappears later for a different reason. On reflexive frames, T3 supplies the global witness condition \(GW\), and Lean verifies the essence-compressed positive collapse theorem
\[
  \operatorname{Refl}(R)+T2^++T3^++G\text{-sup}+CONST
  \Longrightarrow\MCp.
\]
The informative negative collapse schema then follows from the separately machine-checked collapse-channel equivalence under involutive FDE negation.

The finite-model layer provides complementary negative evidence. It regression-checks the T1 positivity-glut obstruction and both T2 countermodels. A broader exhaustive two-world, one-entity \(G,Z\) family retains 873 models satisfying \(A1_L\), positive rigidity, relevant positivity completeness, and relevant exemplification consistency together with the fixed bilateral semantic laws. Every retained model satisfies \(T2^+\). Dropping any one of those four recovery assumptions individually yields a T2 countermodel in that same bounded family. This establishes bounded individual indispensability relative to the generated family, not global model-theoretic necessity.

Gate 8 then shows that the T2 bottleneck is definition-sensitive. The project-internal exact positive Godlikeness condition
\[
  G^+_{\mathrm{exact}}(x)
  \Longleftrightarrow
  \forall\varphi\,
  \bigl(\pos{\varphi(x)}\leftrightarrow\pos{P(\varphi)}\bigr)
\]
internalizes the local positive reflection step. Lean therefore proves
\[
  G\text{-exact}^+ + R^+\Longrightarrow T2^+_{\mathrm{exact}}
\]
without \(A1_L\), \(COMP_P^G\), or \(CONS_G^G\), while an executable model shows that exact positive Godlikeness remains compatible with genuine gluts. The Scott T2 obstruction is therefore not an unavoidable consequence of four-valued essence alone; it depends on the informational strength assigned to Godlikeness.

The Anderson comparison exposes a different strengthening. Its literature-grounded positive interface classifies the necessary properties of an individual through positivity and modifies essence accordingly. The project adds explicit bilateral negative-evidence clauses and machine-checks their classical recovery. A two-world S5 model of this bilateral Anderson candidate has positive necessary Godlike existence while positive modal collapse fails for a contingent formula application. Moreover, the Anderson T3 branch also reduces to symmetry alone, with a corresponding non-symmetric S4-style countermodel. Thus Scott and Anderson share the same currently verified modal return bridge at the T3 stage; their substantive difference lies upstream in how Godlikeness and essence generate the necessary-existence interface.

The Fitting comparison opens a third axis. The mechanized literature treats the intension/extension distinction as central to Fitting's revision, so the project now enforces it directly in Lean: a bilateral extension and a world-indexed intension are distinct types, and the Fitting positivity interface accepts extensions rather than intensions. This is intentionally only a type-level milestone. Fitting essence, necessary existence, negative support, and collapse behavior remain to be reconstructed before a four-valued Fitting theory can be claimed.

The principal open problems are therefore now twofold. For the Scott-support branch:
\[
  \boxed{
  \text{Which weaker principled four-valued assumptions, if any, suffice for }T2^+\text{ globally?}
  }
\]
For the comparative branch:
\[
  \boxed{
  \text{Which structural features of Godlikeness and essence determine collapse across Scott, Anderson, and Fitting lifts?}
  }
\]

Classical recovery has been formalized at the bilateral Scott and Anderson interfaces. Exact higher-order correspondence with the complete AFP/HOL developments, a faithful extension-level Fitting chain, broader paired-neighborhood semantics, and a dedicated prior-art audit remain publication-stage requirements.

The paper continues to distinguish general semantic theorems, machine-checked results, finite countermodels, bounded exhaustive evidence, and open global-minimality claims explicitly.
