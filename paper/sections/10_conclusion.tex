\section{Conclusion and Research Agenda}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This manuscript begins from a deliberately narrow claim. The G\"odel--Scott theory is not merely an object to be translated from two values to four. It is a controlled test case for asking whether classically unitary modal principles conceal distinct channels of positive and negative information.

At version 0.3 five pieces are in place. First, the Scott theory is frozen as a classical control system. Second, the propositional semantics is a conservative Belnap--Dunn/FDE kernel that is paraconsistent, paracomplete, and classically recoverable. Third, the classical modal-collapse proof has been reduced to a dependency spine that identifies A1, A4, D1, T3, and frame conditions as the principal interfaces to inspect. Fourth, the modal layer is fixed by a bilateral relational semantics with full box/diamond duality and classical recovery, together with an exact paired-neighborhood representation. Fifth, the positivity layer has been decomposed far enough to expose three non-classical facts: A1 has two independent directional information flows, strong A1 couples positive A4 rigidity to universal negative rigidity, and the classical local reflection step from God-like exemplification to positivity fails separately through glut and gap mechanisms.

Gate 3 also corrects the target notion of negative modal collapse. The clause \(\neginfo{\chi}\Rightarrow\neginfo{\Box\chi}\) is reflexively trivial on S5 and is therefore discarded as a collapse diagnostic. The informative negative component is instead
\[
  \MCm:\quad \neginfo{\chi}\Rightarrow\pos{\Box\neg\chi},
\]
equivalently \(\neginfo{\chi}\Rightarrow\neginfo{\Diamond\chi}\).

The next step is Gate 4: construct a minimal assumption lattice around the reflection and rigidity bridges and determine which nodes force \(\MCp\), corrected \(\MCm\), both, or neither. In particular, positivity completeness and consistency of relevant God-like exemplification must now be treated as explicit variables rather than hidden classical background assumptions.

The paper will be revised gate by gate. Open sections are intentionally part of the artifact: they make explicit which claims the current theory has not yet earned.
