\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

The project sits at the intersection of two mature lines of work whose combination motivates the present question. First, G\"odel--Scott and related ontological theories have been extensively reconstructed and analyzed with computational tools, including comparisons of Scott, Anderson, and Fitting variants and their behavior with respect to modal collapse \cite{BenzmuellerScott2025,BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019}. Recent machine-checked analysis further separates positivity rigidity from ultrafilter structure as explanations of collapse \cite{Benzmueller2026Collapse}.

The Anderson/Fitting comparison is especially relevant to Gate 8. Anderson's original emendation and Fitting's intensional treatment provide the historical controls \cite{Anderson1990,Fitting2002}. The computer-supported analysis of Benzm\"uller and Fuenmayor and its AFP developments distinguish intensional properties from their extensions and use this distinction to compare the two non-collapsing variants \cite{BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019,FuenmayorBenzmuellerAFP,BenzmuellerScottAFP2025}. In the source IHOML treatment, Scott and Anderson apply positivity to intensional properties, whereas Fitting moves positivity to property extensions and distinguishes de-re from de-dicto formulations. The present project follows that typing discipline rather than modeling Fitting by merely changing axioms over the existing Scott property type.

The four-valued reconstruction introduces an additional issue that is absent from a purely Boolean extension space. If the Fitting property domain is identified with the unrestricted type of all bilateral extensions, that type contains arbitrary glut constructions. In particular, a universal glut extension makes the project's relevant exemplification-consistency condition incompatible with any positive Godlike witness. The admissible-domain reconstruction in Section~\ref{sec:fitting-admissible} should therefore be read as a semantic-domain choice forced by the interaction between Fitting's extensional architecture and unrestricted bilateral comprehension, not as an attribution to Fitting of an historical four-valued restriction. The selected domain remains closed under FDE negation and may contain genuinely glutty extensions.

This also sharpens the de-re/de-dicto comparison. The project reproduces a K-style, frame-free de-re possibility-to-necessity route at the bilateral extensional interface, while the de-dicto route requires an explicit stability bridge connecting the extension of the Godlikeness intension across accessible worlds. The source literature already warns against conflating neighboring de-re and de-dicto formulations \cite{BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019}; the present finite separation model makes that distinction executable in the four-valued setting.

The same literature motivates a methodological distinction in the Anderson branch. Anderson strengthens Godlikeness by classifying the necessary properties of an individual through positivity and modifies essence accordingly, rather than merely adding a stronger Scott-style reflection principle \cite{BenzmuellerFuenmayor2019}. The bilateral negative clauses used here are consequently a project-specific FDE reconstruction around a literature-grounded positive interface; they are not attributed to Anderson himself.

Second, Belnap--Dunn/FDE semantics provides the canonical four-valued distinction between truth-only, falsity-only, both, and neither \cite{Belnap1977,Dunn1976}. Font supplies a foundational De Morgan-lattice analysis, while recent work on upsets and (n)-filters gives a broader algebraic comparison class for four-valued consequence \cite{Font1997,Prenosil2023}. These sources motivate the next filter experiment but do not by themselves identify logical consequence filters with the present higher-order positivity predicate. Modal extensions with Belnapian values have been developed using Kripke and algebraic semantics \cite{OdintsovWansing2010,RivieccioJungJansana2017}. Particularly close in semantic neighborhood is Petrukhin's recent treatment of essence and accident modalities in an S5-based four-valued setting \cite{Petrukhin2026}.

The actualist layer also has an independent source boundary. The AFP control uses existence relativization to restrict individual quantifiers at each world \cite{FuenmayorBenzmuellerAFP}. Work on partial semantics for varying-domain quantified modal logic shows why non-existence creates a genuine semantic choice rather than a harmless notational variant \cite{Johannesson2018}. Neither source is claimed to contain the profile-quotient factorization theorem proved here.

\paragraph{Novelty status.}
A first reproducible prior-art pass now covers exact four-valued/G\"odel queries, the primary Anderson and Fitting sources, the current AFP control development, De Morgan filter literature, and actualist varying-domain semantics. It did not locate a direct predecessor combining all of these components, but a negative search result does not establish absence. The manuscript therefore states structural and mechanization results only. In particular, the symmetry-only T3 reductions, the bilateral Anderson candidate, the unrestricted Fitting-comprehension obstruction, the admissible-domain repair, and the profile quotient remain project results without a global novelty claim.
