\section{From Positivity Rigidity to Modal Collapse}
\label{sec:collapse}

The classical proof structure already identifies a narrow pressure point for the four-valued analysis. Fix a world \(w\), an individual \(x\), and a property \(Z\). In the Scott theory,
\[
  G(x)@w,\; Z(x)@w
\]
forces \(P(Z)@w\): if \(Z\) were non-positive, A1 would make \(\neg Z\) positive, while D1 would then force the God-like \(x\) to exemplify \(\neg Z\), contradicting \(Z(x)\). A4 then yields \(\Box P(Z)\), and D1 propagates \(Z\) to God-like entities at accessible worlds. Thus the local classical spine is
\[
  G(x),Z(x)
  \xRightarrow{A1+D1} P(Z)
  \xRightarrow{A4} \Box P(Z)
  \xRightarrow{D1} \Box\forall z\,(G(z)\rightarrow Z(z)).
\]

Substituting the constant-in-individuals property \(Z:=\lambda z.\chi\) embeds an arbitrary proposition \(\chi\) into this mechanism. Together with T3, which supplies necessarily existing God-like witnesses, and suitable frame conditions, the standard Scott setting yields
\[
  \chi\rightarrow\Box\chi.
\]
The detailed dependency analysis is maintained in \texttt{docs/MODAL\_COLLAPSE\_SPINE.md}. This local structure is consonant with the recent machine-checked diagnosis that positivity rigidity, rather than ultrafilter structure alone, is a principal driver of collapse \cite{Benzmueller2026Collapse}.

\subsection{Correct bilateral collapse targets}

The positive persistence component remains
\[
  \MCp:\quad \pos{\chi}\Rightarrow\pos{\Box\chi}.
\]

The initially considered negative clause
\[
  \neginfo{\chi}\Rightarrow\neginfo{\Box\chi}
\]
is not a useful collapse condition on the fixed S5 frames. Since \(\neginfo{\Box\chi}\) means that some accessible world negatively supports \(\chi\), reflexivity makes this implication valid for every formula independently of Gödel--Scott.

The informative negative component must instead express \emph{universal} persistence of negative support:
\[
  \MCm:\quad \neginfo{\chi}\Rightarrow\pos{\Box\neg\chi}.
\]
Using the bilateral box/diamond duality established in \cref{sec:modal}, this is equivalent to
\[
  \neginfo{\chi}\Rightarrow\neginfo{\Diamond\chi}.
\]
On the classical fragment, this is precisely the falsity-side counterpart of modal collapse: applying the classical schema \(\theta\rightarrow\Box\theta\) to \(\theta=\neg\chi\) yields persistence of falsity across all accessible worlds.

\subsection{Gate-3 obstruction to the local classical bridge}

The local reflection target
\[
  REF^+:\quad \pos{G(x)}\land\pos{Z(x)}\Rightarrow\pos{P(Z)}
\]
already fails under the minimal bilateral interface, even when strong A1 is assumed. The glut and gap counterassignments of \cref{sec:positivity} identify different reasons:

\begin{itemize}
  \item with a glut, the complement forced by A1 can coexist with \(Z(x)\), so the classical contradiction-elimination step disappears;
  \item with a gap in positivity, the classical dichotomy between positive and non-positive properties is unavailable.
\end{itemize}

Consequently, the first collapse experiment cannot be parameterized by A1 and A4 alone. It must explicitly track whether the local reflection bridge is recovered, and if so by which consistency/completeness assumptions.

\subsection{Revised Gate-4 target}

\statusopen

The earlier four-switch matrix \((A1^+,A1^-,R^+,R^-)\) is retired because two of its switches were not genuinely independent: the biconditional A1 channel schemas collapse into one another under substitution, while \(-P\Rightarrow-\Box P\) is reflexively trivial.

The next experiment instead begins with the informative ingredients
\[
  A1_L,\qquad A1_R,\qquad R^+,
\]
together with explicit structural conditions governing the local reflection step, such as positivity completeness and consistency of God-like exemplification. The outputs are the corrected \(\MCp\) and \(\MCm\).

\begin{conjecture}[Bilateral separation]
There is a natural four-valued lift of the Scott theory in which \(\MCp\) and \(\MCm\) are not equivalent.
\end{conjecture}
\statusconjectural

\begin{conjecture}[A1-driven channel coupling]
The two directional components of A1 play asymmetric roles in any derivation that couples \(\MCp\) and \(\MCm\), analogous to their established role in deriving negative positivity rigidity from \(R^+\).
\end{conjecture}
\statusconjectural

Countermodels remain first-class results: failure of either conjecture under a natural lifting would identify a hidden channel coupling and would therefore remain structurally informative.
