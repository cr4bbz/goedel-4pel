\title{Decomposing Modal Collapse:\\A Four-Valued Analysis of G\"odel--Scott Positivity}
\author{Julian Voigt}
\date{Working manuscript, version 0.7}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
I study the inferential architecture of the G\"odel--Scott ontological theory under a bilateral four-valued semantics admitting both inconsistent and incomplete information. Scott's classical theory is fixed as a control system and reconstructed incrementally over a Belnap--Dunn/FDE propositional kernel, bilateral Kripke modality, actualist individual quantification, and higher-order property quantification. The original conjecture that positive and negative modal collapse might separate is falsified under involutive FDE negation: the informative signed collapse schemata are equivalent by substitution. The substantive decomposition instead occurs upstream. Scott's A1 splits into two independent directional information flows with distinct proof-theoretic roles: one supports truth-only positive-property exemplification, while the other participates in the reflection and essence branch. Mere positive support is insufficient for the T1 analogue because positivity gluts absorb the classical reductio. I define support-based bilateral Godlikeness, essence, and necessary existence without fixing a four-valued object-language conditional. Two explicit two-world S5 models show that the natural essence semantics does not make Godlikeness an essence automatically: T2 fails separately through glut and gap mechanisms. A relevant consistency/completeness package is sufficient to recover positive T2. Once positive T2 is available, only positive support for A5 is needed to reconstruct positive T3 in S5, and positive T2 together with positive T3 and constant-property embedding yields modal collapse directly. These central implications are machine-checked in Lean 4.30.0. An executable finite-model oracle verifies the named glut/gap countermodels and, in a broader bounded two-world one-entity family, finds 873 models satisfying the full T2 recovery package, all of which satisfy T2; dropping any one of A1-L, positive rigidity, relevant positivity completeness, or relevant exemplification consistency yields a countermodel in that family. The remaining minimality claim is therefore explicitly global rather than bounded: which weaker principled assumptions, if any, suffice for T2 beyond the verified recovery route?
\end{abstract}
