\title{Decomposing Modal Collapse:\\A Four-Valued Analysis of G\"odel--Scott Positivity}
\author{Julian Voigt}
\date{Working manuscript, version 0.19}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
I study the inferential architecture of the G\"odel--Scott ontological theory under a bilateral four-valued semantics admitting both inconsistent and incomplete information. Scott's theory is fixed as a classical control and reconstructed over a Belnap--Dunn/FDE kernel with bilateral Kripke modality, actualist individual quantification, and higher-order positivity. The original conjecture that the positive and negative modal-collapse channels separate fails for a structural reason: under involutive negation the two informative signed schemata are substitution instances of each other. The decomposition therefore occurs upstream. Scott's A1 splits into two directional information flows with different jobs; two explicit S5 models satisfying the control stack, strong A1, and positivity rigidity refute positive T2 through separate glut and gap mechanisms; a targeted regularity package recovers it; and once T2 is available, symmetry alone suffices for positive T3.

Comparing the Scott, Anderson, and Fitting architectures then shows that these variants relocate the modal work rather than merely weakening the frame. The Fitting case is the sharpest. Identifying the extensional property domain with every bilateral extension makes the relevant consistency package incompatible with any positive Godlike witness, so I reconstruct the candidate over a selected FDE-negation-closed domain of admissible extensions without globally excluding gluts. On that domain the Godlikeness-as-essence route splits in two: a classification route driven by positivity completeness, and a quotient route that factors admissible extensions through positive-property profiles and leaves positivity gaps unresolved. The quotient is the fixed-point condition of a canonical closure operator, and a four-valued \(\delta\)-filter on those fixed points preserves the gap exactly as long as ultrafilter maximality is withheld. Throughout, Lean 4.30.0 carries the general theorems and an executable finite-model oracle carries the countermodels and bounded searches, so unbounded claims and bounded evidence stay separate.
\end{abstract}
