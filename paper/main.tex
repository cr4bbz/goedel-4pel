\title{Decomposing Modal Collapse:\\A Four-Valued Analysis of G\"odel--Scott Positivity}
\author{Julian Voigt}
\date{Working manuscript, version 0.5}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
I study the inferential architecture of the G\"odel--Scott ontological theory under a bilateral four-valued semantics admitting both inconsistent and incomplete information. I fix Scott's classical theory as a control system, adopt a conservative Belnap--Dunn/FDE propositional kernel, and extend it with a bilateral Kripke semantics recovering ordinary modal semantics on the classical fragment. Scott's positivity axiom A1 decomposes into two independent directional information flows, while positive and universal-negative positivity rigidity are independent without A1 but become interderivable under strong bilateral A1. The informative positive and negative modal-collapse schemata themselves do not separate under involutive FDE negation, so the substantive decomposition occurs upstream. I identify a locally minimal recovery package for the classical reflection step from God-like exemplification to positivity and then reconstruct the A2/A3/Godlikeness branch without fixing a four-valued object-language conditional. Necessary entailment is represented semantically, and truth-only positivity is distinguished from glutty designated positivity. Under this distinction, A1-R together with the positive-support lifting of A2 yields a four-valued analogue of Scott's T1: truth-only positive properties are positively possibly exemplified. Mere positive support does not suffice, as witnessed by an empty-actual-domain glut model. A truth-only reading of A3 therefore yields possible positive God-like existence without globally excluding positivity gluts. I freeze a support-based bilateral definition of Godlikeness that recovers Scott D1 classically and discharges the earlier D1+ interface. Full higher-order lambda abstraction also discharges constant-property embedding, whereas global God-like witness supply, positivity completeness, and consistency of arbitrary God-like exemplification remain unforced. The next question is whether the essence and necessary-existence branch supplies the missing witness structure or introduces further four-valued obstructions.
\end{abstract}
